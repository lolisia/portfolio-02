# SD Unity-chan 3D Model Data

© Unity Technologies Japan/UCL

`sd_unitychan.glb` is converted from "SD Unity-chan 3D Model Data" v3.1
(https://unity3d.jp/unity-chan_contents/releaseNote.php?id=SDUnityChan&lang=en),
FBX → glTF with Blender. Texture is downscaled to 1024px and the motion take is split into clips.

## Additional animation

The `Gathering`, `FishingBegin`, `FishingLoop`, `FishingStop` and `Chopping` clips are retargeted and baked from
"Human Crafting Animations FREE" by Kevin Iglesias (Unity Asset Store, Standard Asset Store EULA).
`Chopping` is its `MiningOneHand01_R - Ground` clip (played with an axe prop in the web diorama).
Only the baked clips are included here; the original animation files are not redistributed.
